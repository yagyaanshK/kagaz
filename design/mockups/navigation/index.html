Kagaz · the navigation, put in order

Drawn with the app's own stylesheets, so each system's look is the real one. Devices and counts are made up. The pages follow your system's light or dark setting.

  • Only what you have added. A kind of device (Cameras, Fans…) has an entry once its first device is added, and loses it with its last. Today all seven are listed whether or not anything is set up.
  • By name. Your kinds are in alphabetical order, the same on every device you use, and on Home too.
  • Three parts. Home on top; your devices in the middle; Tools and Settings at the foot, where they stay. Only the middle scrolls when it outgrows the window.
  • Add a device closes the list of your kinds: every kind Kagaz knows is behind it, so nothing is lost by hiding the ones you have not got.

Phones: two ways, yours to choose

A · recommended The bar stays Home, Tools, Settings, as today. Home is the list of your kinds, by name, each one tap away. The bar never changes under your thumb.

B The bar holds your first three kinds itself, and More holds the rest with Tools and Settings. One tap fewer for those three; the bar changes when you add or remove a kind, and Tools and Settings move one level down.
