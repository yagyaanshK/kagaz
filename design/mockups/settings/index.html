Kagaz · settings, put in order

Drawn with the app's own stylesheets; devices are made up. Nothing here is built yet.

  1. A setting lives with the smallest thing it changes: this device, this kind of device, or all of Kagaz. Never in two places.
  2. One door to all of them. Settings lists Kagaz's own few, then one row for each kind you have, by name (the sidebar's list). A kind's row opens the page its gear opens; a device's row opens that device's settings. So every setting is reached from the top, and from the thing itself.
  3. Set once, change where needed. Where it makes sense, a kind holds the setting for all its devices, and a device says "Same as all" until it is given its own. Its own are marked and can be given back.
  4. Few, and sorted. On a page, what holds for all comes first, then each device by name. A group has a handful of rows; what is only for when something is wrong sits under "More".
  5. Find a setting. One field at the top of Settings finds any of them, at any level, and says where it lives.
  6. A row is one line and one control, acts at once, and says so when it could not be saved. What is needed in the middle of something (the viewer's key menu) stays there too, as a shortcut to the same setting.
