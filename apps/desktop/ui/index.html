                Brands

                Kagaz works with Tapo and Xiaomi cameras today; the others are coming.

                Sign in with Tapo

                Your TP-Link (Tapo) account. The password is sent to TP-Link only, the same way the Tapo app does; Kagaz keeps the session token in a private file, never the password.

                Sign in with Xiaomi

                Your Xiaomi (Mi Home) account. Kagaz talks to Xiaomi only, the way Xiaomi's own sign-in page does, and keeps Xiaomi's sign-in token in a private file, never your password.

                Scan it with your phone's camera or Google Lens, or on a Xiaomi phone in Settings, then Xiaomi Account. Xiaomi's sign-in page opens on the phone; signing in there signs in Kagaz.
